# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want YGO Card Scanner to be a welcoming, respectful project for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, language, or the cards they collect. All participants are expected to act with empathy and good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and open to feedback.
- Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy: do not request, publish, or mock another person's collection data or personal information.
- Help keep discussion accessible to contributors with different levels of Android and Kotlin experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or hateful language.
- Personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or disruptive bad-faith conduct.
- Sharing private information, credentials, collection exports, or other data without permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring project safety, privacy, or scope requirements after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove content, limit participation, or take other appropriate action when this code is not followed. Reports will be handled as privately as practical and without retaliation.

## Reporting

While the repository is private, contact the repository maintainer through the private channel used to grant access. After a public release, the maintainer will publish a public reporting route here. Do not use public issues for reports that contain personal or sensitive information.
